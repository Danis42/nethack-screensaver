## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-01 | Tampering | Lua download in the NLE build | medium | mitigate | Keep the existing `URL_HASH SHA256=4f18ddae...` pin in the fork's CMakeLists.txt (unchanged by this plan); a tampered tarball fails the build |
