## Phase Goal

**As** the owner, **I want to** check every progress number against a fresh, reproducible natural-game funnel of `main` and have claims without proof rejected automatically, **so that** every claim of progress toward the ascension can be checked.

<objective>
Reject progress claims without proof automatically (MEAS-09, D-09..D-11, D-18): a stdlib-only script reads the Stop-hook payload, finds progress claims with deterministic patterns, verifies the cited job IDs, full commit hashes and queries against the live database and git, and blocks the reply when a claim lacks valid proof. Every decision is written to an audit log.

Purpose: trust was broken by claims of runs that had not started; the rule "a job counts as started only once its first game row exists" must be enforced, not remembered.
Output: `scripts/check_claims.py`, labelled corpus, `.claude/settings.json` Stop hook, audit-log ignore rule.
</objective>
