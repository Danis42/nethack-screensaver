## Phase Goal

**As** the owner, **I want to** check every progress number against a fresh, reproducible natural-game funnel of `main` and have claims without proof rejected automatically, **so that** every claim of progress toward the ascension can be checked.

<objective>
Replace the `rung` number with a stage-key funnel (MEAS-02): per stage, how many natural games reached it, with a Wilson interval, computed from the stored ladder dict and shown through the API, MCP, CLI and dashboard; decisions use stage metrics only.

The stage list is `autoascend/ladder.py` RUNGS after plan 01-05 added `quest_portal` (D-21): MEAS-02's "Quest portal" is that stage, "Quest" is Home 1.

Scope note: this plan touches 12 files (7 source, 5 test). The MCP tool stays in the tracer, which must reach every surface (API, MCP, CLI, dashboard); 12 is under the 15-file split threshold and each task touches 7 files or fewer.

Purpose: `rung` treats stages as nested and hides what happens past each stage; the funnel is what every later phase reports against.
Output: `bench/funnel.py`, `/api/funnel`, `bench_funnel`, `screens-bench funnel`, dashboard table, `stage_<key>` metrics.
</objective>
