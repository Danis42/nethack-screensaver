**As** the owner, **I want to** check every progress number against a fresh, reproducible natural-game funnel of `main` and have claims without proof rejected automatically, **so that** every claim of progress toward the ascension can be checked.

Purpose: plan 01-02 built a valid test; this makes it the only way an experiment can reach a verdict.
Output: scheduler, server, MCP and CLI changes; ref validation in gitrefs.
