## Phase Goal

**As** the owner, **I want to** check every progress number against a fresh, reproducible natural-game funnel of `main` and have claims without proof rejected automatically, **so that** every claim of progress toward the ascension can be checked.

<objective>
Turn "seeds 0..N-1 of a commit" into explicit seed windows keyed by the full game identity (sha, seed, lgen mode, env fingerprint, replicate), and record the environment of every job (MEAS-07, the infrastructure for MEAS-05, the job-recording half of MEAS-01).

Purpose: the baseline, the A/A and every experiment need named seed windows that keep dev, confirm and final apart, game rows that know which NLE build played them, and replicate rows that never double-count.
Output: `bench/seeds.py`, store schema 2, scheduler/server/MCP/CLI changes. The range refusal policy (D-07, D-17, D-22) is the next plan, 01-10, split off to keep each plan within budget. The lgen dispatch uses the worker protocol of plan 01-05, hence the dependency.
</objective>

## Interface contract (defined here, used by plans 01-11 .. 01-16)

Job spec (baseline): `ref`, `sha` (40 hex), `subject`, `games` (= hi - lo), `seeds = {"range": "dev" | "confirm", "lo": int, "hi": int}` (half-open), `lgen = "off" | "seed"`, `replicate = 0 | 1`, `gate` (str or null; enforced by plan 01-10, D-22), `env_id`, `env` (fingerprint dict), `lock_blob`.
Job spec (experiment): candidate/baseline fields as today plus `seeds`, `lgen`, `gate`, `env_id`, `env`, `lock_blob_candidate`, `lock_blob_baseline`.
Game key: `(sha, seed, lgen IS NULL or NOT NULL, env_id, rep)`; `lgen` column holds the actual NLE lgen value, NULL means off.
