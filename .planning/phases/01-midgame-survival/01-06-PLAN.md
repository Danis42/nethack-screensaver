<objective>
Measure what the seeded bench can actually see, and tune it once before any baseline is stored.

Purpose: MEAS-01's intent and the phase goal ("the bench can see 1-3 point effects"). The exact
A/A proves replay but has a zero-width interval (RESEARCH Pitfall 2). Resolution depends on how
often paired games disagree (q): RESEARCH sizing says q 0.15-0.25 gives a 2.2-2.9 point half-width
at 1152, barely better than the unpaired 3.7. Levers (RESEARCH Open Questions 2 and 4, resolved
here with the researcher's recommendation since the owner delegated design): seed level
generation separately (`lgen`), and/or add a 2304 checkpoint. Both change every later verdict, so
they are decided now, before the baseline (nothing seeded is stored yet besides the A/A).
Output: measured q / half-width / MDE for two variants, the decisions, and their application.
</objective>
