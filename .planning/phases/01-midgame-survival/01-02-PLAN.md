<objective>
Remove the last wall-clock and hash-seed influences on a bench game.

Purpose: MEAS-03. The live Sokoban solver stops on `time.monotonic()` after 10 s, so a game that
reaches Sokoban (rung 4+, about 9% of games) can play differently under bench load (RESEARCH
Pitfall 6). Measured during planning on this machine with no wall-clock limit: exhausting the
default 300,000-state budget took 51 s (soko1-1 with its spare boulders removed) and 89 s
(soko3-2 likewise), so simply dropping the deadline would trip the screen watchdogs; the budget
must shrink to what the old 10 s allowed. Pinning `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` for workers is defence in
depth (RESEARCH Pitfall 7: no hash dependence found, one line).
Output: deterministic live solver budget, a hash-seed pin, tests for both.
</objective>
