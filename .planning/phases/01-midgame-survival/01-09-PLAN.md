<objective>
Rest to full HP before exploring on (SURV-01), measured.

Purpose: the human soldier-ant study found survivors met the ant at full HP in 100% of cases vs
80% of the dead (PORTING.md, CONTEXT specifics). Today the bot only heals by searching once nothing
is left to explore (`global_logic.py` 848-849, to 80%). Resting costs food: about 1 HP per 4-15
turns at XL 1-9 (RESEARCH Pattern 4), and the dropped Elbereth variant saw more fainting deaths
(Pitfall 10), so the rest is gated by food, capped per level, and its turns are counted.
Ordered right after starvation (Killer order, D-07, recorded in plan 01-08).
Output: `autoascend/rest.py`, the strategy wiring, counters, tests, and one judged experiment.
</objective>
