# Part 1a: item skills

Goal: the bot can use the items the ascension route needs (ASCENSION-PLAN-NH5.md gaps 3-9). Each skill is
proven on a wizard-mode staged start (nethack_screensaver/staging.py) that hands the hero the item. Route-stage
use (crossing water at Medusa, the passtune at the Castle) comes in Parts 1b and 1c and reuses these primitives.

Rules hold in natural play too. They fire only for identified items (shop price-ID, altar, wizard ^I), so
early play changes little. A no-harm bench check against the main baseline closes the plan.

## Decisions (owner delegated design, 2026-11-02)

- D-01 Staged item tests: `Stage` gets `level=None` (stay on Dlvl 1, no teleport) and `identify` (False keeps the
  kit unidentified to test price-groups). Check = end-of-game snapshot inventory (worn/on-hand text) plus result.
- D-02 Rings: wear only identified, harmless-when-cursed types, ranked: slow digestion, free action, poison
  resistance (until intrinsic), fire, cold, shock resistance, see invisible, sustain ability, protection and
  increase damage only if enchantment known >= +1. Never auto-wear: teleportation, polymorph, levitation, hunger,
  aggravate monster, conflict, invisibility, regeneration (hunger cost), adornment, searching, warning (no use to
  the bot). Unknown B/U/C: allowed (none of these types hurts when cursed); keep one hand free for levitation.
- D-03 Amulets: life saving > reflection (if no worn reflection) > ESP > versus poison (until resistant) >
  guarding > magical breathing. Never: strangulation, restful sleep, change, unchanging, flying (Part 1f).
  Unknown B/U/C allowed for these types.
- D-04 Armour by property: value = AC + bonus: magic resistance +10, reflection +8, speed boots +5, gauntlets of
  power +3, oilskin +2, helm of telepathy +1, displacement +1, elven cloak +1, water walking +1. Never wear:
  levitation boots (worn only by the levitation skill), fumble boots, gauntlets of fumbling, helm of opposite
  alignment, dunce cap. The unknown-B/U/C armour rule (AC-GAP-NH5.md) stays for Part 4.
- D-05 Unicorn horn: apply a non-cursed horn when confused, stunned, hallucinating, blind (not by blindfold) or
  sick, before waiting it out or praying.
- D-06 Scrolls: read identified enchant armour (worn armour, none above +3), enchant weapon (wielded, below +5),
  remove curse (an equipped item is cursed), identify (pick the first unidentified ring/amulet/wand). Unknown
  scrolls read only by price group: 20 = identify; 60 unlabeled excluded = enchant weapon; 80 = enchant armour or
  remove curse (both safe). Never confused, never in Sokoban. Read-testing unpriced scrolls = Part 4 experiment.
- D-07 Wish list (WISHES-NH5.md): by what is missing: magic resistance (blessed greased +2 gray dragon scale mail),
  reflection (blessed amulet of reflection), levitation (blessed ring of levitation), then 2 blessed potions of
  gain level. Castle-specific gain-level order is Part 1c.
- D-08 Pry chests: merge exp/pry-chests (ffe4685; exp 26 neutral, starved +1.7 [-0.7, +4.1]) as a capability
  (Castle wand chest); covered by the closing no-harm check.
- D-09 Levitation and instruments: primitives only here (put on/remove a levitation item and know when floating;
  play a tune by notes). Their route use is tested in 1b/1c.
