<objective>
Stuck cause #1 (DESC-03, DESC-02).

**Rule (fixed 2026-10-25, before the numbers):** take the games of the Current baseline that fail `free5`;
for each, the bucket of its last stuck report (02-03). Shares among those games:
- `stairs-unreachable` >= 35% -> tactic **clear-way**: when stuck with a seen but unreachable `>`, remove
  the obstacle the reports name most for that bucket (closed/locked door: kick it, except on the Minetown
  level and shop doors; boulder: push it from the reachable side along the shortest path, or force-fight it
  if a pick or force bolt is held). New: the old ideas searched for hidden passages; this acts on a known
  obstacle between the bot and a known `>`.
- else `stairs-reachable` >= 35% -> tactic **take-it**: a reachable `>` was not taken; find why from 5
  replayed rows (milestone target elsewhere, `dive_stairs` filtering it out, stair destination memory) and
  fix that one cause.
- else `no-way-found` >= 35% -> tactic **escape-cheese** (CONTEXT D-06): when stuck with no frontier and no
  `>`: read unidentified scrolls one per 50 turns (teleportation or magic mapping are common), then jump into
  any known hole or trap door. New: none of the dropped ideas used items or traps.
- else -> tactic **frontier-first**: when stuck with frontier > 0, walk to the nearest frontier square
  directly, ignoring the exploration scoring, until it is gone.
Output: rule application in PORTING.md, `exp/stuck-1`, verdict on `free5`.
</objective>
