# Online research (2026-10-29)

Searches: NetHack 5.0 strategy/food/Valkyrie, 3.7 death drops, supply chests, bot/LLM ascensions.

- Food death drops were cut on purpose (commit 39a7dc1c, "too easy now"); a wiki forum thread
  (Forum:Hunger and Poison, May 2024) says that "as long as you keep moving" corpses are enough
  from the Mines on, and that wandering empty levels is the usual cause of starvation. Matches our
  data: 39% of starvations happen on a level the bot is stuck on (FOOD-NH5.md) -> experiment 23.
- Potion of healing in 3.7/5.0: much more common, heals more (8+4d4 uncursed), has a unique price
  (price-identifiable); quaffing extra healing at full HP raises max HP (+2, blessed +5).
- Valkyrie 5.0: spear (Basic, can reach Expert in spear), Excalibur much harder for non-Knights;
  community calls the role nerfed, no 5.0-specific strategy guide yet (wiki: "Strategy
  developments still in progress").
- 2026 LLM agents (kenforthewin blog, BALROG harness on NLE): no ascension, deepest Dlvl 10;
  failures were reactive food handling, descending too early, taking the Mines stairs unprepared,
  not finding hidden stairs. The reported GPT "Astra" dwarvish-Valkyrie ascension (37,140 turns)
  rests on one blog post (korben.info); no primary write-up found.
- AutoAscend (our base) won NeurIPS 2021 with a behaviour tree; no other bot ascended 3.6.

Sources: nethackwiki.com Death_drop, Forum:Hunger_and_Poison, Forum:Role_Difficulty_in_3.7.0,
Potion_of_healing; gamebrief.net NetHack 5.0.0 guide; kenforthewin.github.io nethack-agent;
korben.info ai-finished-nethack-barely; github.com/skbly7/autoascend.
