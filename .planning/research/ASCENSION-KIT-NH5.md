# Ascension kit: what NetHack 5.0 changes (for Phases 3-8)

Source: wiki "Ascension kit" (written for 3.6), wiki "NetHack 5.0.0" and "Dragon scale mail", checked
against reference/nethack-5.0.0 (objects.h, do_wear.c). 2026-10-27.

| Kit item | NetHack 5.0 |
|---|---|
| Gray / silver DSM | unchanged: magic resistance / reflection only |
| Other DSM colors | second extrinsic while worn: orange +free action, yellow +stoning res, black +drain res; gold DSM is new |
| Unicorn horn | still cures ailments; no longer restores lost attributes |
| Wand of wishing | 1 charge, recharges only to 1:1; Castle chest also holds a potion of gain level (Sanctum escape) |
| Amulet of Yendor | grants a wish when picked up |
| Scroll of scare monster | no longer works on @ humans or unique monsters |
| New items | amulet of flying (Plane of Air instead of levitation), amulet of guarding, helm of caution, wand of stasis |
| Speed | wand of speed monster only temporary; potion of speed grants intrinsic speed less often: speed boots matter more |
| Luck / artifacts | sacrifice luck capped by monster value; Excalibur much harder for Valkyries (no starting long sword) |
| Hazards | anti-magic fields deadlier with magic resistance; Gehennom is "hot" (potions on the floor break) |
| Healing | natural HP regeneration lower at high levels; more potions of healing early |
